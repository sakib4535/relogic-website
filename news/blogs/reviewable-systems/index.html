Generative systems that stay reviewable

Notes from shipping copilots and agents with permissions, evaluation and a human in the loop.

A generative system that cannot be reviewed is not ready for work that matters. Relogic builds copilots and agents with three non-negotiables: a permission model, an evaluation set, and a human stop.

Permissions decide which sources the model may touch. Evaluation decides whether an answer is good enough to show. The human stop decides when the system must hand the work back.

What “agent” means here

An agent is not a demo that clicks around a screen. It is a workflow with tools, logs and a known failure mode. If we cannot say what it does when the retrieval is empty, it does not ship.

This is the same standard used in Relogic’s startup and enterprise research pathways.
