Command, not dashboard clutter

A short brief on FounderCMD and why founder tools fail when they try to be every app at once.

Founders do not need another dashboard that lists every metric they already ignore. They need a command surface: what is true this week, what is blocked, and what decision is waiting.

FounderCMD is built around that narrower job. Status, cash, pipeline and the next action sit in one place. Integrations exist to feed the command view, not to recreate Slack, mail and accounts inside one window.

The test

If a founder cannot answer “what needs me today?” in under a minute, the tool is noise. We design against that failure.
