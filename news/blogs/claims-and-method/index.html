Keep claims tied to method, source and uncertainty

How Relogic structures research intelligence so a finding can be traced back to the study design instead of a slide.

A claim that cannot be walked back to a method is marketing. Relogic treats research as a chain: question, design, source, estimate, uncertainty, and only then a sentence a decision-maker can use.

Most decks skip the middle of that chain. The result looks confident and falls apart in review. We keep the graph of the work visible — which study, which cohort, which missing data — so a reader can disagree with a finding without guessing how it was made.

What we write down

Every engagement starts with the question in one line, the population or system in one line, and the claim we are not allowed to make. That last line saves more time than any model.
